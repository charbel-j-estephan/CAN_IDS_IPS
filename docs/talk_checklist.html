StepSwitchExpected

7SW1 downrefused: maintenance mode is off
8SW1 upaccepted, new version, LEDR4 / LEDR5 switch slot; the nine steps the FPGA did
9SW1 uprefused: older version (rollback)
10SW1 uprefused: stamp does not match; 1 bad stamp counted
11SW1 downport closed; the model stays through a power cut

On every result, point at two lines: attack frames that reached the car (0 when protected) and normal frames lost (must be 0).
