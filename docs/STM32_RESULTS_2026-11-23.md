Notes
- ilate=0 and dlate=0 in every run. cmax ranged 10408 to 14359 cycles (about 62 to 85 us at 168 MHz). The worst case is close to the 86 us budget for 8 byte frames.
- "Attack destroyed" counts frames lost on the bus. Few flags are needed because the attacker goes bus off early in each block and its later frames never reach the bus.
- Genuine losses in T1 (frames 2532 to 2536) and T5 (frames 2798 to 2829) sit inside a retry burst right after an attack block starts.
- T2 failure: genuine 0x608 frame 03 7F E1 80 FF (from the Fuzzy capture) gets 4 of 5 tree votes for Fuzzy offline too. The IPS destroyed every retransmission and the sender went bus off. Strict mode would pass it.
- T4 failure: first flagged genuine frame was 0x43F 01 45 60 FF 6C. Same chain, sender bus off after 32 flags.
- The sender firmware does not recover from bus off, and it carries every genuine ID. In a car only the one ECU would go bus off, and it would retry after recovery and be destroyed again.
- T5 sender timing degraded (late=25, maxlate 6397 us) from USB load at 200k frames. Not an IPS effect.

## Firmware 18, guarded vote rule (same model rf5-d9-p5-2ddda531)

Rule: destroy if unknown ID or 5 of 5 trees vote attack (strong). Destroy if 3 or 4 trees vote attack AND a payload byte is outside the per ID range from the normal TRAINING split (weak). Weak verdicts on one identical frame are capped at 4 in a row. See python/export_ranges.py and mcu/stm32_ids/src/classify.c.
